## Assumptions and method

1. Attack-arm policy ladder is N, S0, W1, W2, W2P, W3: N has no privacy instruction or enforcement, S0 is the frozen instruction-only boundary, and W1/W2/W2P/W3 remove the instruction and add exactly one enforcement layer (S1 pattern redaction, S2 strict static allowlist, S2 configured allowlist, S3 contextual grants). The lawful and benign arms keep S0, S1, S2, S2P, S3.
2. Denominators are evaluable attempts: rows with status 'exception' (harness/infrastructure failures) are excluded from every n and annotated as 'exc'; rows with status 'error' remain in the denominator as intent-to-treat model outcomes (a failed attempt is never counted as a success, and its error is reported in the robustness section).
3. Pooled tests pool all evaluable rows of the relevant arm and policy over all stimuli that have data; if the two groups cover different stimulus sets the test is annotated with a coverage note rather than being silently treated as a clean stimulus-matched comparison.
4. The primary attack outcome is leak_confirmed (mock-cloud receiver receipt); leak_outbound is the secondary outcome. Task completion uses task_completed. Lawful utility uses lawful_cloud_delivery; redaction_marker_in_cloud measures S1's lossy redaction of a legitimate phone share.
5. The two-sided Fisher exact p-value is the sum of hypergeometric table probabilities for all tables no more likely than the observed table, with an inclusive relative tolerance of 1e-7 (so exact ties count); this matches common library behaviour and gives p ~= 0.0007 for 0/10 vs 8/10.
6. The odds ratio is (a*d)/(b*c); it is reported as the string 'inf' when the denominator is zero and the numerator is positive, as 0.0 when the numerator is zero but the denominator is positive, and null when both products are zero (undefined).
7. Wilson score intervals use z=1.959963984540054 with no continuity correction; rates are proportions in [0, 1] (rendered as percentages in Markdown).
8. The Bonferroni family is exactly the seven pooled tests of the updated design: attack N-S0, N-W1, W1-W2, W2-W2P and W2P-W3 (leak_confirmed), plus lawful S2-S3 and S2-S2P (lawful_cloud_delivery), with alpha = 0.05/7. The P_phone-only and C_phone-only attack subsets and the ancillary lawful-redaction test are reported raw and explicitly marked as outside the family.
9. Cells or tests without evaluable data are marked not evaluable, never imputed; such family tests keep their place in the fixed family size of seven but produce no raw or corrected decision.
10. Records whose status is missing default to 'unknown' and stay in the denominator (only the literal status 'exception' is excluded); any non-'completed' status is visible in the robustness section.
11. Row labels outside the current design (for example stale attack-arm S1/S2/S3 evidence directories, or N rows in the control arms) are retained and shown exactly as recorded in the per-cell tables and flagged in the robustness section, but are never used by the pre-specified tests.
12. Ordering is deterministic everywhere (arm, stimulus, policy via fixed rank tables, then attempt/repetition); no wall-clock timestamps are embedded, so regenerating from the same evidence yields identical files.
